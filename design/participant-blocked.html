Note
Expired, revoked and never-existed all render as this one message, on purpose. Telling them apart would let someone guess which trip codes are real by watching which error they get back — an enumeration leak. There is also deliberately no "try again" here: retrying a dead link forever is a worse experience than one honest sentence and a next step. Temporary failure and rate limited are the two cases that genuinely are worth a retry, so they look and read differently on purpose.
