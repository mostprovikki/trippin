## Identity-change proposals

1. **§2: an archived trip is read-only** except Settings notes/actuals/photo links and Unarchive.
   Not written down today; H1 needs it as the rule.
2. **§4: does the 44px floor cover text links** (nav, "Open itinerary", person names)? M4.
3. **§5: per-row Delete** — at rest, or under the row's `⋯`? L2.
4. **Packing items assignable?** If yes, M1 is a picker; if no, the Overview stops showing names on them.

## Not proposing

- Checklists badge 3 vs "Before tomorrow · 2 open": different cut of the data (all open vs due before tomorrow), allowed by §6.
- No trip DELETE route: archive is the lifecycle; deleting trips is an owner scope call.
- Empty /search hint, Before-tomorrow row alignment: too small to file.
- Smoke #13 (Settings Archive/Clone): already `trip-planner-1ow`.
