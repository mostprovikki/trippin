What this sends, and what it never does

Used to draft

  • Aggregated preference counts — "2 vegetarian, 1 vegan, 3 no restriction," never whose
  • Trip-level parameters — duration, destination, season, group size
  • The budget's shape — category totals and basis notes, no per-person overrides

Never leaves the deployment

  • Names, phone numbers, emails
  • Emergency contacts, medical notes, allergies
  • Document numbers, or the documents themselves
