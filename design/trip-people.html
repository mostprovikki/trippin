About access links

A link is the only way someone without an account gets into their piece of the trip. No password, no account — just a token in the URL.

  • Tokens are stored hashed, the same way a password would be. Nobody — including you — can look up or re-display an existing link.
  • That's why the raw link is shown to you exactly once, right after it's created — and why the recovery for a lost, revoked or expired link is always the same: generate a new one.

Why this order

Whoever is blocking the trip most sits at the top — an expired passport and a dead link outrank a routine renewal. §2.6 asks for actionability, not a percentage — there is no readiness score anywhere on this page.
