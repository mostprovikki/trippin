Why eight, always

Every trip in Tripper carries the same eight categories so budgets are comparable across trips — there is no "add a category" control anywhere in the product. A category this trip has no cost for isn't forced to invent one: leave it undrafted (hatched, italic, "Not drafted") or set it to a real ₹0 if you've actually confirmed it costs nothing. The two must never look the same.
