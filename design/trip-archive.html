Un-archive Sri Lanka 2025?

This is not a plain toggle back to how things were. Three separate things happened when this trip was archived, and un-archiving treats each one differently:

  • The 5 access links are gone for good — un-archiving does not bring them back Links are stored hashed, not in plain text, so there is no original to restore. If you want people to have access again, you re-issue a fresh link one person at a time — each one shown to you exactly once, the same as issuing a new link on any other trip.
  • The frozen itinerary, budget estimates and checklists unlock and become editable again 8 days · 34 items · 8 categories · 22 packing items and 7 tasks, all locked since 14 Feb 2025. If all you want is to record what actually happened, you don't need this at all — actuals, notes and photo links are editable right now, archived or not.
  • The recorded actuals and retrospective notes are untouched either way The ₹2,59,300 already logged and the 3 notes on file stay exactly as they are, whether this trip is archived or not.
  • Status goes back to Active The same status it held the day it was archived — 8 days that already happened, now open for editing again.
You can archive it again afterwards — that's the Archive again action, and it isn't blocked once it's un-archived. The five original links still won't come back.
