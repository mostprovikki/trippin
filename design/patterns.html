Consequences & reversals

Every one-way-feeling action in Tripper, in one place, so it's checkable as a system rather than twenty separate inventions.

Nothing on this page is a real control — every dialog below is rendered inline, in the page, not as an overlay, using the exact .modal__panel, .damage and .reassure markup that appears for real on trip.html and trip-dates.html. Every entry names where it actually lives so you can go open the real, clickable version. The reason this page exists at all: read in isolation, each warning looks like its own little invention. Read together, they're one small set of rules, applied consistently.

Genuinely destructive

Something concrete is gone afterwards and nothing in the product brings it back on its own — a link, a file, a row, 41 itinerary items. These earn a hard stop: an itemised .damage list, and the worst of them a typed phrase before the button even lights up.

Merely decided

A choice that can be re-opened — a status, a date, a destination, an applied draft. Big consequence is not the same thing as destructive. Un-deciding a destination touches a whole itinerary and budget, and still belongs here, because nothing is deleted — the trip just goes back to describing something that isn't settled yet. These get a plain explanation and, where the reversal is genuinely free, a .reassure note instead of a warning.

The reassuring inverse · the same visual family, pointed the other way

Half the point of warning loudly about the eleven things below is that everything not on that list can be committed to without flinching. A .reassure note that over-dramatises a safe change is its own kind of failure — so it's built to look like calm, not like a smaller warning.

Settled is not sealed. Dates, destination and every applied draft can still be changed — each one will tell you what it costs first. trip.html — a fully-ready trip
An idea is allowed to be vague. Nothing is overdue, missing or wrong — this is what a trip looks like six weeks before anyone commits. trip.html — an idea-stage trip
Confirmed isn't a one-way door. Plenty of trips move twice before they happen. trip-dates.html — confirmed dates
This is fully reversible and destroys nothing. Move it forward again whenever you like. trip.html — stepping back

Delete "Bhutan someday" for good?

Lives at trip.html → an empty trip's lifecycle

Deleting is only offered because nothing has been attached to this trip. Everything that would go:

  • The name, and nothing else 0 participants · 0 dates · 0 destination candidates · 0 budget figures · 0 itinerary days · 0 links ever issued.

The moment any of those counts stops being zero, this button disappears and archive becomes the way to put a trip away — deliberately, so a trip somebody has put work into is never one click from gone.

Revoke Lena Hoffmann's link?

Lives at trip-people.html → access links → revoke

Her link is active, and expires on its own in 12 days anyway — this ends it now instead.

  • It stops working immediately, for her specifically Not the trip, not everyone else — just Lena's one link.
  • It cannot be un-revoked Tokens are stored hashed, not in plain text, so there is nothing to restore — re-issuing is the only way back, and it's a new secret, shown once.

Daniel Weiss already has a link — make a new one anyway?

Lives at trip-people.html → access links → new link over an existing one

  • His current link dies the instant the new one is created Silently, from his side — if he still has the old URL open, it just stops working. He needs the new one to get back in.
  • Everything he already entered on the old link stays The link is how he gets in, not where his profile and documents live.

Reopen the date decision?

Lives at trip-dates.html → confirmed → reopen

The one dated-decision action that is destructive — not moving the dates, but taking back the decision itself. Confusingly named the same way "un-decide" is for a destination, but the two behave differently: see the rollback comparison further down.

  • All 10 itinerary days and the 41 items in them are removed This is the expensive part — it cannot be undone by re-confirming the same dates. Copy anything you want to keep first.
  • The trip drops from Confirmed back to Planning Status is worked out from the facts, so it follows automatically — see the rollback section below.

Move the dates of a trip that already has ten days of plan

Lives at trip-dates.html → confirmed → move the dates · covers both "shift vs re-date" and "shift an active trip"

This extra banner only appears when the trip is Active. Five people are already reading the itinerary on their phones — whatever you choose, they'll see it on their next refresh with no warning and no message from the app.

One dialog, two completely different intents — pick the one you mean:

Slide the whole trip Merely decided Day 3 stays Day 3, it just falls on a different date.
  • All 41 items survive, on all 10 days
  • Stays Confirmed — still exact dates
Re-date it Destructive Days are rebuilt from the new dates — for when the shape changes, not just the timing.
  • Days 8–10 and their 11 items stop existing
  • The 15 Nov goal falls outside the trip entirely

Advance the trip's status

Lives at trip.html → lifecycle → move forward — and it has no dialog at all

Moving a trip forward — Idea → Planning, or the automatic Confirmed → Active on departure day — asks nothing of you, because nothing is lost by admitting a trip is further along than it was:

Idea → Planning "Moving to Planning asks nothing of you — it just says out loud that this is happening."
Confirmed → Active "It becomes Active on its own on 6 Nov — you don't have to do anything."

Push the departure back to Confirmed?

Lives at trip.html → lifecycle → step back

For when departure slipped — not the same as editing the dates.

  • The itinerary, budget and checklists are untouched Status is a label on the trip, not on its contents.
  • All five access links keep working Only archiving revokes links.
This is fully reversible and destroys nothing. Move it forward again whenever you like.

Make 10–14 Oct 2026 the trip's dates?

Lives at trip-dates.html → promoting a window · same dialog reached from "Confirm these ten days" in the slight-variation mode

This is the answer, not another option — here's what it starts:

  • Five itinerary days appear, empty to begin with Days can't be added or removed by hand — they are these dates.

Mark Alleppey as the decided destination

Lives at trip-destination.html → comparing candidates → decide

Deciding is exclusive — marking one candidate un-marks any other — but it isn't guarded, because un-deciding is always one click away. The only rule with teeth: a decided candidate can't be deleted without first un-deciding it.

Un-decide Vietnam & Cambodia as the destination?

Lives at trip-destination.html → decided → un-decide

The reference case for "big consequence isn't the same as destructive": a lot depends on this, and nothing gets deleted.

  • The 10-day itinerary and the 8-category budget were built around this place They stay exactly as they are — "9 nights × 3 twin rooms" doesn't rewrite itself — but they now describe a destination the trip no longer claims to have decided.
  • Vietnam & Cambodia goes back into the comparison, not into a bin Re-deciding it is one click, identical to how it was chosen the first time.

Un-archive Sri Lanka 2025?

Lives at trip-archive.html → un-archive — the hardest reversal in the product

"Reversal" undersells it: three different things happen, and only two of them actually reverse.

  • The frozen snapshot becomes editable again Itinerary, budget estimates and checklists — exactly as they were frozen, back to Active.
  • Recorded actuals and retrospective notes are untouched They were editable before un-archiving and stay that way after — archiving never locked them.
  • The six links archiving killed do not come back That residue belongs to "archive," in the destructive group above — un-archiving reopens the trip, not the hashed tokens. Re-issuing is per person, one at a time.

Change the trip's currency from INR to EUR?

Lives at trip.html → trip details → change base currency

34 amounts are already entered. Two honest options, no default that pretends this is free:

Keep the numbers, change the label Recommended Every figure stays as typed. The total reads €4,80,000 instead of ₹4,80,000 — obviously wrong where it's wrong, so it's findable.
Convert them at today's rate The total becomes €4,403 at 1 EUR = 109.01 INR — but reasoning notes like "9 nights × 3 twin rooms × ₹4,000" don't convert, so a note and a euro figure stop matching.
If you only want to read the trip in euro, you don't need this — the "View in" lens changes nothing and can't corrupt a figure.

Apply the AI budget draft over Stay, hand-edited 2 days ago?

Lives at ai-review.html → applying a draft → overwrite

Said before the click, not discovered after — the whole point of this row:

  • Your note on Stay, entered 2 days ago, is replaced Along with its estimate — the assistant's new figure and reasoning take its place.

Status rollback · the rule that decides whether editing dates costs you Confirmed

Status is never a label you set — it's worked out from whether its requirements still hold. Confirmed means "the group has final dates." The rule that decides what survives an edit is precise, and — this is the part that matters — announced inside the action's own warning, before you click it, never discovered afterward.
