Un-decide Vietnam & Cambodia?

This isn't a small edit. A ₹4,80,000 budget and a 10-day, 41-item itinerary were built around this exact choice. Here is exactly what happens and what doesn't:

  • Nothing is deleted automatically The itinerary's 10 days, the budget's 8 categories and the checklists all stay exactly as they are — un-deciding a destination never cascades into deleting content, unlike reopening the date decision does.
  • Everything downstream keeps talking about a place that's no longer decided Day 3's "Overnight on a Ha Long junk", the budget's "Cambodia e-visa fees" line, the checklist task "Get Cambodia e-visas" — all of it stays worded for Vietnam & Cambodia until you either pick a new destination or edit these by hand.
  • The trip can no longer stay Confirmed Confirmed needs both dates and a decided destination. Without a destination it drops to Planning until you decide again — you're told this before the click, not after.
  • All 3 candidates come back into the comparison, none of them marked Including Vietnam & Cambodia itself — re-deciding it is one click.
Nothing is destroyed here. This reopens a decision — it doesn't erase the work built on it.

Vietnam & Cambodia can't be deleted while it's decided

A decided candidate is the trip's actual destination — a ₹4,80,000 budget and a 10-day itinerary already assume it. Deleting it out from under that would leave both pointing at nothing, so it's not offered.
